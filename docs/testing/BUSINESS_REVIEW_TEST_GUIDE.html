Roles and responsibilities

Administrator

Manages Employee accounts and may publish, review history, and roll back governed Knowledge Documents.

Standard User

Consults vendor and General Flow knowledge, browses allowlisted documents, and manages only their own Conversations.

Coordinator

Creates synthetic accounts, records IDs/versions, coordinates the optional Railway redeploy, and confirms rollback.
